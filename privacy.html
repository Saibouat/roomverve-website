Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

RoomVerve keeps your room photos and designs on your phone. We send a photo to our AI providers only to check it and to create your designs, and we do not keep it afterwards.

Who we are

RoomVerve is run by Saibou Ouattara, a sole trader trading as RoomVerve, 7 Bushberry Court, 91 Wellington Road, Enfield, EN1 2PW, United Kingdom. We are the controller of your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Contact: hello@roomverve.co.uk.

What we collect and why

Data Why we use it Legal basis How long we keep it
Your room photo To check the photo, give advice and create your four designs Contract (to provide the service you asked for) Not stored by us. Sent to our providers for that request only
Your choices (room type, style, walls, how much change, ideas you include) To create designs that match what you chose Contract Not stored by us. Sent with the request only
Your network (IP) address To limit the number of free checks and designs per network each day, and to prevent misuse Legitimate interests (keeping the service fair and affordable) A daily counter, deleted after 30 days
Purchase details (subscription or one-room purchase, an anonymous app user ID) To unlock what you paid for and restore purchases Contract As long as you use RoomVerve, then as required for tax and accounting records
Messages you send us To answer you Legitimate interests Up to 2 years

We do not ask for your name, email or address in the app, we do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Your photos, your four designs, your guide's advice, your shopping lists and your language choice are saved on your phone only. We cannot see them. You can delete one room, several rooms or all of them in Settings → Your data. Deleting the app also deletes them.

Who we share data with

We use these providers to run RoomVerve. They act on our instructions and only for this purpose:

Provider What it does Where
Supabase Runs our server functions and the daily usage counter London, UK
Anthropic (Claude) Checks your photo and writes your guide's advice. Under its commercial terms it does not use our requests to train its models, and deletes them automatically within 30 days United States
Black Forest Labs (FLUX) Creates your four designs from your photo. Its standard API terms allow it to use the images it receives and creates to improve its AI models Germany and the United States
Google Play / Apple App Store Takes payment for subscriptions and purchases Under their own privacy policies
RevenueCat Manages your subscription status (an anonymous app user ID and your purchases, not your photos) United States

Where a provider is outside the UK, we rely on its data processing terms and the UK's approved safeguards for international transfers to protect your data. Please do not include faces, documents or other personal details in your room photos.

Shopping links

The shopping list opens a web search for each item. When you tap a link you leave RoomVerve, and the website you visit has its own privacy policy. We do not earn from these links.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to move it to another service. Most of your data is only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself in Settings. To make a request, email hello@roomverve.co.uk. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).

Children

RoomVerve is for people aged 16 and over, including students. We do not knowingly process data of children under 16. Purchases by under-18s are made through Google Play or the App Store, under their family and payment settings.

Security

Photos are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our secret keys stay on our server and are never in the app.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters to you.
